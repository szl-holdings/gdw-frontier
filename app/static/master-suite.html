02 / Interpret

Know what the evidence can say.

Strategy proposes.

Mutation evidence enters modeled localization and replay. Strategy identities have no control path for changing protected risk parameters.

Governance decides.

Local, expiring HMAC manifests qualify paper or shadow transitions. Invalid signatures, expiry, or parent mismatch fail closed within the modeled service.

Authority stays bounded.

Even a passing modeled risk gate does not submit to a broker. Regulatory assessment, market access, canary and live authority remain outside this suite.
